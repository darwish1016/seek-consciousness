Within seconds, a profound awakening changed the entire course of my life.

I felt a direct connection with God or Source and experienced the oneness of everything, held within a pure, infinite, all-encompassing love.

I knew immediately that this was what I had been searching for my entire life.

Since then, I’ve spent the majority of my free time exploring every path, practice, teaching, and catalyst I could find that might help me go deeper within and reconnect with the divine.

I created Seek Consciousness because I want you to have the same opportunity to discover this for yourself through direct experience.

I built the map I always wished I had, so you can find your own way.

Start Here

A map for the inner journey.

Seek Consciousness brings together the most important practices, teachings, tools, and catalysts I’ve found for exploring consciousness—and learning how to bring what you discover into everyday life.

I’ll be drawing from ancient wisdom and the essential knowledge people preserved and passed forward for us, along with modern understanding and what I’ve learned through direct experience.

Together, we’ll explore some of the deepest questions we can ask:

What is consciousness?

Who are we beneath our thoughts and identity?

What happens when we die?

What is our place in the universe?

And how do we access our own connection to a higher power?

The Blueprint is here to help you see how these pieces connect—not to tell you which path you’re supposed to follow.

You decide what you explore, how deep you go, and what you leave alone.

The spiritual path is unique to each one of us. As you become more in tune with yourself and your spirit, your own direction begins to reveal itself more clearly.

The point is not to give you someone else’s answers. It is to help you discover what feels true through your own direct experience.
