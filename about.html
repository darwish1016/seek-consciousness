About Seek Consciousness

A living project.

Seek Consciousness is an ever-evolving project built from a lifetime of searching, direct experience, and trying to understand how the strongest pieces fit together.

I don't believe one religion, tradition, teacher, practice, or catalyst holds the entire picture. I'm interested in what works, what holds up, and what can help us move deeper down our own path.

Seek Consciousness isn't about accumulating everything.

It's the distillation of what remains after we remove what isn't essential.

Every week, I'll keep learning, refining what I understand, and adding new pieces to the larger picture.

Getting to follow what fascinates me and share what I discover with you is one of the greatest honors of my life.

When I look back on my time here, the measure that matters most will be how many people I was able to reach, help, and empower.
